About Nuer SDA Hymnal

Purpose:The Nuer SDA Hymnal App is a digital worship companion designed to preserve and share the rich hymn traditions of the Nuer Seventh-day Adventist community. Built with care and authenticity, it brings together faith, culture, and technology in one accessible platform.

The Vision: This app makes worship more immersive and inclusive for Nuer-speaking believers worldwide. Whether at home, in church, or on the go, the hymnal is always within reach—helping families, choirs, and congregations connect with God through music.

About Author: Gatbel Duop Chol – IT professional and digital innovator passionate about merging faith, culture, and technology. Born in Akobo, South Sudan, my mission is to preserve and share Nuer worship traditions through accessible technology.

"Connecting faith and culture through Nuer hymns, anytime, anywhere."
